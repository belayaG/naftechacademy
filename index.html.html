Certification readiness

We don't just finish the syllabus. We get you ready for exam day.

Every certification track is built around the official exam objectives, then reinforced with practice, review and a readiness check before you book your exam.

Our honest promise: results depend on your own preparation and exam performance, so we focus on measurable readiness — not guarantees no provider can make. Exam vouchers are included only where a program's terms say so.

About the academy

Built by practitioners who've done the work

NAF Tech Academy was founded by Belay and Wakil, who bring backgrounds in cybersecurity and software engineering and industry experience with Fortune 500 companies. They started the academy to make career-focused technology education practical, accessible and connected to real jobs.

Together with a team of professional instructors, we teach not only how a technology works, but how it's actually used on the job — with clear explanations, guided practice and individual support.
